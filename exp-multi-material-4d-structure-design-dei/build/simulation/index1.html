The Self-Closing Gripper Mechanism

The Kutzbach–Grübler criterion F = 3(n−1) − 2j₁ − j₂ predicts whether a printed linkage is locked solid, moves with one controlled degree of freedom (F=2, a working gripper), or is an underconstrained mechanism.
