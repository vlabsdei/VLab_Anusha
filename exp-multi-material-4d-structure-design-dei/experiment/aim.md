### Aim of the Experiment
To design and analyse multi-material 4D-printed structures that transform themselves from a flat, printed state into a functional folded shape, across five mechanics of self-folding:
1. **Stiffness-Contrast Hinges:** Compute the stiffness contrast SC = E<sub>panel</sub>/E<sub>hinge</sub> and the panel rigidity EI = E&middot;b&middot;h<sup>3</sup>/12 to decide whether a fold localises crisply in a soft living hinge or smears across the rigid panels.
2. **Mechanism Mobility:** Apply the planar Kutzbach-Gr&uuml;bler criterion F = 3(n&minus;1) &minus; 2j<sub>1</sub> &minus; j<sub>2</sub> to a self-closing gripper and separate the locked, rigid, working (F=2) and floppy regimes.
3. **Fold Kinematics:** Run forward kinematics (x<sub>tip</sub> = &Sigma;L<sub>i</sub>cos&Sigma;&theta;<sub>j</sub>, y<sub>tip</sub> = &Sigma;L<sub>i</sub>sin&Sigma;&theta;<sub>j</sub>) on a serial hinge chain, and solve the inverse problem for the fold angles &theta;<sub>i</sub> that land the tip on a target point.
4. **Origami Tessellation:** Characterise the rigid Miura-ori fold - mountain-valley angle tan&theta;<sub>M</sub> = tan&alpha;&middot;sin&phi;, pack ratio cos&phi;&middot;&radic;(1&minus;sin<sup>2</sup>&alpha;sin<sup>2</sup>&phi;), and the negative (auxetic) Poisson's ratio &nu;<sub>x</sub>.
5. **Living-Hinge Fatigue:** Estimate cyclic hinge life from flexure strain &epsilon;<sub>max</sub> = h/(2R) and Basquin's power law &sigma; = &sigma;<sub>f</sub>&middot;N<sup>&minus;b</sup>, then apply a safety factor to find the dependable number of open/close cycles.
