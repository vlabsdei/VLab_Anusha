Sub-Calc C: Folding a Flat Sheet into a Target Shape

A flat printed strip has three soft hinges. As they fold, its tip traces out a 3D shape. Forward: you set the fold angles and watch where the tip lands. Inverse: you place a target and the lab solves the fold angles needed to reach it - exactly the angles you would print into the hinges.
