Sub-Calc A: The Self-Folding Flat-Pack Box

A flat sheet is 4D-printed with soft living hinges running across stiff panels. When triggered, it folds itself into a box - no assembly. Whether the corners come out crisp depends on how much stiffer the panels are than the hinge. Your job: pick a recipe whose fold stays in the hinge so the box snaps up cleanly.
