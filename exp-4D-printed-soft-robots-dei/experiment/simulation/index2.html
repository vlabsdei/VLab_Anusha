Sub-calc C · Friction-Asymmetry Gait

Crawling Locomotion

A peristaltic worm nets displacement only from friction asymmetry: δ_net = δ_ext·(1 − 1/μ_asym) with μ_asym = μ_back/μ_fwd. When μ_asym = 1 the robot just stretches and recoils in place; below 1 it crawls backward.

Live Insight: Set the segment strain and directional friction, then run the gait to watch the worm advance, stall, or reverse.
