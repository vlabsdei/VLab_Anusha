Sub-calc A · Pressure to Bending

Pneumatic Bending

Air pressure in an eccentric cavity bends a strain-limited elastomer finger: M = P·A·d drives curvature κ = M/(E·I), so θ = κL. Past ~30° geometric stiffening makes the finger saturate below the linear prediction.

Live Insight: Set the drive pressure and finger geometry, then run the actuator to curl it to the solved bending angle.
