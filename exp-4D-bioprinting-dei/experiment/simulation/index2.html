Cell Viability Through Print Shear

Extrusion squeezes living cells through the nozzle. Wall shear stress τwall = η·(4v/R) rises steeply as the nozzle narrows, and survival falls as S = exp(−k·τwall·tres). This is the resolution–viability trade-off unique to bioprinting.
