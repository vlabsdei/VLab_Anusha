Bioink Viscosity & the Printability Window

A good bioink is shear-thinning: thick at rest to hold its shape, thin under the nozzle so it extrudes without crushing cells. The power law η(γ̇) = K·γ̇(n−1) sets whether the ink lands in the 1–100 Pa·s printable window at each nozzle.
