Thermal Gelation Kinetics

A printed layer must gel fast enough to support the next one before it slumps. Isothermal gelation follows Avrami kinetics α(t) = 1 − exp(−k·tm); the gel point is where conversion reaches ½ and the storage modulus G′ builds toward its plateau.
