Scaffold Degradation & Mechanical Lifetime

A tissue scaffold must dissolve as native tissue grows in — too fast and it fails early, too slow and it blocks regeneration. Mass decays as M = M₀e−kt, and because a porous strut network scales as E ∝ M4/3, stiffness falls faster than mass.
