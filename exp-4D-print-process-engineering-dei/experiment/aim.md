### Aim of the Experiment
To model and quantify the process-physics that decides whether a fused-deposition 4D print is sound, using five sub-calculators that each isolate one failure mechanism of the print process:
1. **Inter-Layer Bonding by Reptation:** Predict the healing degree D<sub>b</sub> = (t<sub>avail</sub>/t<sub>rep</sub>)<sup>&frac14;</sup> of a polymer weld from nozzle temperature, layer height and print speed, and convert it to a bond strength &sigma;<sub>bond</sub> = D<sub>b</sub>&middot;&sigma;<sub>bulk</sub> against the 47&nbsp;MPa PLA bulk.
2. **Crystallisation During Printing:** Solve the Newtonian cooling curve T(t) of a deposited road, find its dwell t<sub>x</sub> in the T<sub>g</sub>&ndash;T<sub>m</sub> growth window, and use Avrami kinetics X<sub>c</sub> = X<sub>c,max</sub>(1&minus;e<sup>&minus;(t<sub>x</sub>/t<sub>half</sub>)<sup>n</sup></sup>) to get the crystallinity, the stiffening E = E<sub>a</sub>(1+k&middot;X<sub>c</sub>), and the shape-memory recovery penalty.
3. **Residual Stress & Warpage:** Compute the mismatch strain &epsilon;<sub>mis</sub> = &alpha;&Delta;T, the constrained biaxial residual stress &sigma;<sub>res</sub> = E&alpha;&Delta;T/(1&minus;&nu;), the corner warpage &delta; = C&middot;&epsilon;L&sup2;/2h, and test whether &sigma;<sub>res</sub> exceeds the inter-layer bond and peels the part off the bed.
4. **Print Anisotropy (Raster Angle):** Apply the Tsai-Hill off-axis criterion to a unidirectional FDM lamina to map strength &sigma;<sub>&theta;</sub> against raster angle, evaluate the anisotropy ratio AR = &sigma;<sub>L</sub>/&sigma;<sub>T</sub>, and locate the shear-dominated weakest angle &theta;<sub>min</sub>.
5. **Dimensional Accuracy & Tolerance Stack-up:** Separate the systematic shrinkage mean &delta;<sub>mean</sub> from the random process spread &sigma;<sub>proc</sub>, combine three feature errors by RSS and worst-case, and judge capability with C<sub>pk</sub> = (USL&minus;&mu;)/(3&sigma;<sub>proc</sub>) against the 1.33 target.
