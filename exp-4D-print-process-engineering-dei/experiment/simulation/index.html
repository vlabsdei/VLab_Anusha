Neck Growth & Inter-Layer Bonding

Two deposited roads weld by polymer chain interdiffusion (reptation), not metallic sintering. The healing degree D_b = (t_avail/t_rep)¼ sets how much of the 47 MPa bulk strength the interface recovers — and whether the part is isotropic.
