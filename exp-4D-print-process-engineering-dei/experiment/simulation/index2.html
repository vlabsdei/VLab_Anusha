Residual Stress

Constrained cooling locks in biaxial residual stress σ_res = EαΔT/(1−ν). Differential contraction curls the plate (δ = εL²/2h); when σ_res beats the inter-layer bond, the part peels off the bed.

Live Insight: Set print and bed temperature, layer thickness and part length, then read residual stress, corner warpage, and whether it delaminates.
