Sub-calc B · Cooling & Crystallisation

Crystallisation

A freshly deposited road cools by Newtonian convection; the time it lingers in the T_g-T_m window sets crystallinity X_c = X_c,max(1−e−t_x/t_half). More crystallinity means a stiffer part but poorer shape-memory recovery - a genuine 4D trade-off.

Live Insight: Set nozzle and chamber temperature and road diameter, then read crystallinity, stiffness gain, and the recovery penalty.
