Sub-calc D · Tsai-Hill Off-Axis Strength

Raster Anisotropy

FDM is a unidirectional composite: Tsai-Hill gives off-axis strength σ_θ. Strength is lowest near 45-55° where roads shear apart, not at 90° - the anisotropy that governs where a 4D part folds.
