## Indian engineering lab manuals

8. **Aurora's Engineering College - Engineering Physics Lab Manual.** *13. Torsional pendulum*, PDF page 83 (printed page 82). Backs the rigidity modulus n = 8*pi*M*R^2*L / (a^4*T^2), obtained by timing the torsional oscillations of a disc hung on a wire. That n is the shear modulus G which this experiment converts with E = 3G to get the rubbery modulus in epsilon = sigma/E_rubbery.
   [Open the manual (PDF)](https://www.aurora.ac.in/images/pdf/departments/humanities-and-sciences/engg-phy-lab-manual.pdf) - local copy: `screenshots_references/manuals/exp8_engphy_aurora.pdf`, screenshot: `screenshots_references/exp01_modulus__aurora-engphy_rigidity_p83.png`

   Partial fit: the manual proves the modulus measurement only. The WLF shift factor and the Tg relaxation model used in this simulation are textbook theory and are not printed in this manual.
