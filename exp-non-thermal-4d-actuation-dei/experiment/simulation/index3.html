Sub-Calc D: The Wireless Coil Driving an In-Body Robot

A printed robot is implanted in the body and driven wirelessly by a magnetic field from an external coil. The deeper the implant, the harder the coil must work because tissue soaks up the field. Your job: design a coil that delivers enough field at the implant's depth without drawing too much power - and spot when a tiny on-board battery would be smarter.
