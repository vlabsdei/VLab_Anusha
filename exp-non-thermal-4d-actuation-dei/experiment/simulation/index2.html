Sub-Calc C: The Magnetic Micro-Gripper / Catheter Tip

A flexible tip is 4D-printed with tiny magnetic particles baked in. Bring an external magnet close and the tip bends to steer - letting a surgeon guide a catheter or close a micro-gripper from outside the body, with no wires. Your job: pick a particle loading that steers with a gentle, safe magnet.
