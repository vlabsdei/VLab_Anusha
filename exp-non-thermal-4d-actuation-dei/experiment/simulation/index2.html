Sub-calc C · Magnetic Torque

The Magnetic Micro-Gripper / Catheter Tip

Ferromagnetic particles printed into a soft tip align with an external field, generating a magnetic torque τ = m × B that bends the tip. More particle loading means a weaker (safer, more practical) external field is needed to steer it.
