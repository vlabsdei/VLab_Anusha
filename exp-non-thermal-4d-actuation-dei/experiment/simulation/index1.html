The NIR Skin-Activated Implant

Gold nanorods absorb 808 nm near-infrared light — a wavelength that passes through skin and tissue relatively unattenuated — and convert it to local heat, triggering a thermoresponsive implant without wires. Higher power heats faster, but the hot zone spreads wider.
