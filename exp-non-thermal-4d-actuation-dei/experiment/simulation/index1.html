Sub-Calc B: The NIR Skin-Activated Implant

A small device is 4D-printed and placed under the skin. To switch it on, a near-infrared (808 nm) laser shines through the skin and warms a nanoparticle spot on the implant - no surgery needed. Your job: pick a dose that activates the implant while keeping the hot spot tiny so nearby tissue stays safe.
