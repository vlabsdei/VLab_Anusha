Sub-Calc A: The Light-Driven Actuator Strip

A flat strip is 4D-printed with a light-sensitive azobenzene dye. Shine UV (365 nm) on it and it bends toward the light; switch to visible (450 nm) and it relaxes flat - a tiny actuator with no motor and no wires. Your job: pick a light recipe that bends it fast.
