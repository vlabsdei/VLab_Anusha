## Indian engineering lab manuals

8. **IIT (ISM) Dhanbad, Department of Chemical Engineering - Chemical Kinetics and Reaction Engineering Lab Manual (CHC 304).** *Experiment-03: Isothermal Batch Reactor (Determination of Activation Energy)*, page 15.
