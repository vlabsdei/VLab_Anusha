The pH-Triggered Drug Capsule

Weak-acid groups on the capsule shell ionise as pH rises past the polymer's pKa (Henderson–Hasselbalch), pulling in water via Donnan osmotic pressure and swelling the shell open — releasing its payload once past the stomach.
