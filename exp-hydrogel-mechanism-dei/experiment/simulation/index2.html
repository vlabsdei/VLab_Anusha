Body-Temp Valve

PNIPAM-type hydrogels collapse sharply at their Lower Critical Solution Temperature (LCST, ∼32°C) as hydrogen bonds to water break down. A valve built from it snaps shut crossing body temperature - sharpness and swollen size are both tunable.
