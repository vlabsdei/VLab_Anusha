The Body-Temperature Valve

A soft valve is 4D-printed from PNIPAM, a gel that holds water when cool but suddenly squeezes it out near 32°C. While cool the gel is swollen and props the valve OPEN so fluid flows; warm it toward body temperature and it collapses and SNAPS SHUT. No electronics - it senses temperature on its own. Your job: see how sharply it switches right around body heat.
