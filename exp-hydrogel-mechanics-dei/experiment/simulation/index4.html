The Drug-Eluting Implant

A drug-eluting implant is 4D-printed and placed in the body, where it slowly leaks its drug over hours to days. How fast it empties - and whether the dose stays steady - depends on the polymer's release speed and a release-shape exponent. Your job: pick the shape so the implant gives a steady, long-lasting dose. (The model is only trustworthy up to the first 60% released.)
