The pH-Triggered Drug Capsule

A drug capsule is 4D-printed from a pH-sensitive polymer. In the stomach's acid it stays sealed shut; once it reaches the less-acidic intestine the shell swells and the two halves spring open, spilling the drug exactly where it's needed. Your job: pick a polymer whose pKa makes it open in the intestine, not the stomach.
