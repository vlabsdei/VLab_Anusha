The Cartilage Scaffold

A tissue scaffold is 4D-printed to replace damaged cartilage. To work, it must feel as soft as real cartilage - too stiff and it grinds the joint, too soft and it collapses. A probe presses the scaffold to measure its stiffness: the softer the gel, the deeper the dent. Your job: tune the print so its stiffness lands in the cartilage window (0.1-1 MPa).
