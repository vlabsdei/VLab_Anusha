### Aim of the Experiment
To investigate the thermodynamic, mechanical, and electro-thermal behaviors of Nitinol (Ni-Ti) Shape Memory Alloys (SMAs) through five distinct physical principles:
1. **Martensite Phase Transformation:** Evaluate composition-dependent phase transition temperatures (M_s, M_f, A_s, A_f) and plot the martensite phase fraction (x_M) vs temperature.
2. **Stress-Strain & Hysteresis Loops:** Contrast the mechanical response of Nitinol in its shape memory regime (below A_f) and superelastic regime (above A_f), calculating energy dissipation and damping capacity (Q<sup>-1</sup>).
3. **Electro-Thermal Joule Heating:** Model the time-temperature response of current-actuated SMA wire, calculating the activation time (t_act) to reach A_f and identifying safe operational limits.
4. **Actuator Stroke & Sizing:** Size and configure an SMA wire actuator (length, diameter, pre-strain) to meet specific stroke and force requirements for a soft robotic gripper.
5. **Fatigue Life & Cycling Stability:** Analyze cyclic fatigue life using the Coffin-Manson relationship and evaluate the degradation of recoverable strain over repeated actuations.
